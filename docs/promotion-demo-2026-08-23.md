# 2026-08-23 Promotion Demo Outline

## 3-minute flow

1. Enter one parcel address and show verified site facts.
2. Compare the recommended, profitability-oriented, and legal-maximum planning options.
3. Use Quick Plan to add or resize a floor, then switch to Expert Edit to show the same live plan.
4. Open the feasibility view and explain Low / Base / High cost and profit ranges.
5. Show expert handoff items and finish with the evidence-backed verification report.

## Core message

ParcelGrid is not only a building calculator. It connects parcel facts, planning alternatives, financial feasibility, expert handoff, and an evidence-backed report in one decision flow.

## Required cautions

- Distinguish official facts, user-entered assumptions, and algorithm recommendations.
- Describe unverified regulations as review items, not confirmed legal conclusions.
- Describe AI imagery as concept visualization, not permit drawings or construction documents.
- Describe cost and acquisition estimates as decision references, not appraisals or contractor quotations.
